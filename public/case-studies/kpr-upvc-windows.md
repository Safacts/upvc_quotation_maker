# Case Study: KPR UPVC Windows & Door Systems
## How KPR Reduced Quotation Time by 60% with Vitharn ERP

---

### The Challenge

KPR UPVC Windows & Door Systems, a leading UPVC fabricator in Hyderabad, was struggling with:

- Manual quotation creation taking 30–45 minutes per quote
- Excel-based tracking leading to lost follow-ups
- No visibility into quote status or payment tracking
- Difficulty sharing professional quotations with customers

### The Solution

KPR adopted **Vitharn ERP** — the complete UPVC business operating system:

- **Mobile App** — Site staff measure windows and create quotes on-site
- **Desktop Console** — Office staff track all quotations, customers, and payments
- **WhatsApp Sharing** — One-tap quote sharing with customers
- **Tally XML Export** — Clean data flow to their accountant
