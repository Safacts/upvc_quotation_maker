This policy states when a paid pilot fee may be refunded. It does not limit any non-excludable right available under applicable law.

## 1. What the fee covers

The fee covers the agreed pilot access, onboarding, and support boundary for the Pilot Period. It is not payment for a guaranteed quotation volume, approval, conversion, cost saving, uptime level, WhatsApp delivery, accounting result, or production rollout.

## 2. Customer cancellation before access

If the Customer cancels in writing before credentials are issued and before onboarding work begins, Vitharn will refund the amount received less any clearly itemised, pre-approved third-party charge that cannot be recovered. The refund request must identify the payment reference and UPI destination.

## 3. Vitharn cancellation or material non-delivery

If Vitharn cancels the Pilot for a reason within its control, or materially fails to provide the contracted supported web-first workflow and does not cure that failure within a reasonable period after written notice, Vitharn will refund the unused pro-rata portion of the Pilot fee. A security, integrity, or recovery gate failure may cause Vitharn to stop the Pilot immediately and issue the applicable unused-fee refund.

## 4. Customer cancellation after access

The Customer may terminate under the Pilot Contract. After access or onboarding begins, no refund is due solely because the Customer changes its mind, does not use the Service, dislikes a non-material screen change, or does not achieve a commercial result. Vitharn may nevertheless approve a discretionary refund in writing; it is not a precedent.

## 5. Exclusions

No refund is due for incorrect Customer data, unsupported devices or workflows, third-party outages, internet failure, Customer breach, unlawful use, credential sharing, failure to attend onboarding, or a request for an uncontracted feature. These exclusions do not remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 6. Request and processing

Submit a written request to `vitarn.dev@gmail.com` within 14 days after the event relied upon. Include the Contract reference, Customer name, payment date/reference, reason, and UPI details. Vitharn will acknowledge within 3 business days and decide within 7 business days after receiving enough information. Approved refunds will be sent to the original payer’s UPI/bank method within 10 business days, subject to payment-provider processing.

## 7. Data after refund

A refund does not transfer Customer data or Vitharn intellectual property. The Customer should request an export before deletion. After the agreed export window and any legally required retention, Vitharn will delete Pilot data under the Contract.
