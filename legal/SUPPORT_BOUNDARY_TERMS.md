## 2. Included support

Included support is limited to:

- onboarding the agreed pilot users;
- explaining the supported web-first quotation workflow;
- reproducing and triaging defects in the supported workflow;
- reasonable configuration help for agreed branding, customers, products, and pricing;
- guidance on supported PDF generation, share recording, export, and follow-up status;
- reasonable data-export assistance at pilot end; and
- written release or limitation notes where a known issue affects the Pilot.

## 3. Priority guide

| Priority | Example | Customer action |
|---|---|---|
| P0 / critical | suspected cross-tenant access, exposed credential, material data loss, or unsafe status mutation | Email immediately with “P0” and do not continue testing the affected path |
| P1 / high | supported login, save, PDF, or share path unusable for the pilot | Include user, time, quotation/customer reference, and steps to reproduce |
| P2 / normal | non-blocking defect or incorrect display | Include screenshot and expected/actual result |
| P3 / request | training question, enhancement, or new module | Record for later consideration; not a defect commitment |

Vitharn may reclassify a report after investigation. Security reports should not include passwords or secrets.

## 4. Outside the boundary

Support does not include custom development, accounting or tax advice, data entry on the Customer’s behalf, recovering data the Customer never saved or exported, third-party WhatsApp/Tally/payment support, device repair, internet troubleshooting, universal Android/offline support, or guaranteed response, uptime, delivery, conversion, approval, savings, or revenue outcome. Enhancements require a separate written scope, price, and acceptance criteria.

## 5. Customer obligations

The Customer must nominate an account owner, keep credentials private, use supported browsers, supply reproducible evidence, avoid personal or unnecessary sensitive data, maintain its own exports, test business-critical totals, and promptly report suspected privacy or security issues. Vitharn may pause a request that exceeds the cap, is abusive, lacks required information, or creates a security risk.

## 6. Incident handling

For a suspected P0 event, Vitharn may disable a route, user, tenant, or Pilot access to contain risk. Vitharn will communicate material findings and next steps when reasonably verified. The Customer must preserve relevant timestamps, screenshots, quotation references, and browser details, and must not publicly disclose an unverified incident while investigation is active.

## 7. Changes and acceptance

The Pilot is evolving. Vitharn may change non-material screens, workflows, or limits with notice. A material reduction to the contracted supported path will be discussed with the Customer and may qualify for the remedy in `REFUND_POLICY.md`. Continued use after notice means the Customer accepts the updated boundary, unless it terminates under the Pilot Contract.
