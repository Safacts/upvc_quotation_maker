## NOTES FOR IMPLEMENTATION

1. **Product name:** Use "Vitharn UPVC" as the product name. The company is Vitharn (brand), Vitharn ERP Services is the category, Vitharn UPVC is the specific product.

2. **Pricing page link:** The "See Pricing" and "View Pricing" buttons should link to `/upvc/pricing`. The pricing page already uses the warm rust/orange theme — this landing page should match that same design system.

3. **Google Sign-In privacy note:** This is deliberately placed in the hero (not buried in a footer). UPVC fabricators are often cautious about digital tools. Addressing privacy upfront builds trust.

4. **Trust badges:** The three numbers from the pricing page hero (₹0 monthly fees · 7-day trial · 24-hour setup) should be displayed as small stat pills somewhere visible on scroll — either below the hero or above the features section.

5. **Language level:** Written at roughly a Grade 6-7 reading level. Short sentences. No jargon like "end-to-end," "ecosystem," "seamless," or "synergy." If a word wouldn't be used in conversation with a shop owner, it doesn't belong here.

6. **WhatsApp sharing:** Not a dedicated feature card because it's only available on Next+ and Final tiers. Mentioned in the features section subtext and in Card 1's body as a sharing option.

7. **GST note:** The pricing page FAQ says "We currently don't charge GST on our software." This landing page doesn't need to repeat that — it's a pricing-page detail. The landing page focuses on the customer's GST invoicing (which Vitharn supports).
