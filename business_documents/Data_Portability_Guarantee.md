# Data Portability Guarantee — Vitharn ERP Services

## Our Promise to You

We guarantee that YOUR business data is ALWAYS yours. If you ever stop using Vitharn — for any reason — you can export ALL your data in standard, usable formats within 24 hours.

## What We Guarantee

### 1. Full Data Export (Anytime, Anywhere)
At any point during or after your subscription, you can download:
- All quotations (PDF, CSV, XLSX, Tally XML)
- All customers (CSV, XLSX)
- All products (CSV, XLSX)
- All payments/ledger (CSV, XLSX)
- All photos/attachments (ZIP archive)
- Complete audit trail (CSV)

### 2. Export Formats
- **CSV** — Opens in Excel, Google Sheets, any database
- **XLSX** — Native Excel format with formatting
- **PDF** — Professional format for records
- **Tally XML** — Direct import into Tally Prime
- **ZIP Archive** — Everything in one download

### 3. Data Ownership
- You own 100% of your data
- We never claim ownership of your business information
- We never sell, share, or monetize your data
- Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit

### 4. Backup Independence
- You can request a full backup at any time (no charge)
- We provide automated monthly backup reminders
- Backups are stored independently of our infrastructure
- You can restore from backup even if our systems are down

### 5. Transition Support
If you move to another provider:
- We provide 30 days of free export assistance
- We help map your data to the new system's format
- We guarantee zero data loss during transition

### 6. Source Code Escrow (Optional Premium Feature)
For Enterprise clients (₹55,000+):
- Source code deposited with a third-party escrow service
- If we cease operations for 90+ days, escrow releases source to you
- You can continue running the system independently
- Annual escrow fee: ₹5,000 (maintained by Vitharn)

## Legal Standing
This guarantee is legally binding under Indian Contract Act, 1872.
It survives termination of the service agreement.
Disputes resolved in Hyderabad courts.

## How to Export
1. Login to your Vitharn account
2. Go to Settings → Data Export
3. Select format and date range
4. Click "Download"
5. Your data is yours — forever
