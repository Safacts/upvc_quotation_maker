## 1. PURPOSE & SCOPE

This Refund & Cancellation Policy ("Policy") sets out the terms under which Vitharn ERP Services provides refunds, processes cancellations, and handles service termination. It applies to all five pricing tiers: **Low, Base, Next, Next+, and Final**.

Vitharn ERP Services is committed to **fair and transparent** dealings. This policy is designed to protect both the client and the agency.

---

## 2. REFUND POLICY

### 2.1 Cooling-Off Period (7-Day Money-Back Guarantee)

Vitharn ERP Services offers a **7-day cooling-off period** from the date of service activation ("Activation Date"). During this period:

- The Client may request a **full refund** if they are unsatisfied with the service for any reason.
- No questions asked. No justification required.
- The refund will be processed within **7 business days** of the refund request.
- The refund will be made to the **original payment method** (UPI only).

**Conditions:**
- The 7-day period starts from the **date the client receives access credentials** (not the date of payment).
- Refund requests must be made in **writing** (email to `vitarn.dev@gmail.com` or WhatsApp).
- The Client must **not have exported data** in a usable format (CSV, XLSX, or Tally XML) prior to requesting a refund. If data has been exported, the refund may be denied as the service has been substantially consumed.

### 2.2 Refunds After the Cooling-Off Period

After the 7-day cooling-off period:

- **No refunds** will be provided for the one-time setup fee.
- This is because Vitharn has already incurred costs for: instance setup, branding customisation, data migration, configuration, and onboarding.
- The **one-time fee is non-refundable** after the cooling-off period.

### 2.3 Partial Refunds (Pro-Rata for Annual Services)

For clients on **annual update subscriptions** (₹5,000/year after the first free year):

- If the Client cancels before the end of the subscription period, a **pro-rata refund** may be provided for unused months.
- Refund amount = (₹5,000 ÷ 12) × remaining unused months.
- Partial refunds are processed within **14 business days**.

### 2.4 Refund Exceptions

Vitharn ERP Services reserves the right to **deny a refund** if:

1. The Client has **violated the Client Services Agreement** (e.g., shared credentials, reverse-engineered the software, used the service for illegal purposes).
2. The Client has **exported all their data** in a usable format (indicating the service was substantially used).
3. The refund request is made **more than 30 days** after the cooling-off period has expired.
4. The Client has a **history of fraudulent chargebacks** or abuse of the refund policy.

### 2.5 How to Request a Refund

To request a refund:

1. Send an email to `vitarn.dev@gmail.com` with the subject line: **"Refund Request — [Client Name/Company]"**
2. Include:
   - Client name and company name
   - Invoice number or payment reference
   - Date of payment
   - Reason for refund request (optional during cooling-off period)
3. Vitharn will respond within **2 business days** with confirmation and next steps.
4. Refunds are processed via **UPI transfer** to the original payment source within **7 business days** of approval.

## 3. CANCELLATION POLICY

### 3.1 Client-Initiated Cancellation

The Client may cancel their Vitharn service at any time by providing **30 days' written notice** (email or WhatsApp). Upon cancellation:

1. **Data Export:** Vitharn will provide a **complete one-time data export** within **7 business days** of the cancellation request, covering:
   - All quotations
   - All customer records
   - All product catalog entries
   - All payment records (Final tier)
   - Format: **CSV** (all tiers) and **Tally XML** (Final tier only)

2. **Service Suspension:** Vitharn will **not** suspend service immediately. The Client retains full access during the 30-day notice period to export their data and transition their workflow.

3. **Data Deletion:** After the 30-day notice period expires, Vitharn will **permanently delete all Client data** from its servers within **30 days**, with **written confirmation** provided to the Client on request.

4. **No Refund:** Cancellation after the 7-day cooling-off period does not entitle the Client to a refund of the one-time setup fee.

### 3.2 Vitharn-Initiated Termination

Vitharn ERP Services reserves the right to **terminate the service** immediately if:

1. The Client **breaches the Client Services Agreement** (e.g., non-payment, violation of terms).
2. The Client uses the service for **illegal, fraudulent, or harmful purposes**.
3. The Client engages in **abusive behaviour** towards Vitharn staff.
4. Continued provision of service becomes **technically or legally unfeasible**.

In such cases:
- Vitharn will provide **7 days' written notice** before termination (except in cases of illegal activity, where immediate termination may apply).
- The Client will still receive a **data export within 7 days** of termination.
- **No refund** will be provided for breach-of-contract terminations.

### 3.3 Non-Payment Suspension

If an invoice remains unpaid beyond **30 days** from the due date:

1. Vitharn will send a **payment reminder** (first notice).
2. After **45 days**, Vitharn will send a **final notice** warning of suspension.
3. After **60 days**, Vitharn may **suspend service access** until payment is received.
4. After **90 days**, Vitharn may **terminate the service** and delete all Client data.
5. **Data export will still be provided** upon request within 30 days of termination, even for non-payment cases.

**Important:** The Client's data is never held hostage. Even in cases of non-payment, Vitharn will provide a data export before deletion.

## 5. UPGRADE & DOWNGRADE TERMS

### 5.1 Upgrades

- The Client may **upgrade to a higher tier** at any time.
- The Client pays the **price difference** between their current tier and the new tier.
- Example: Base (₹25,000) → Final (₹55,000) = additional ₹30,000.
- New features are activated within **24 hours** of upgrade payment.

### 5.2 Downgrades

- **Downgrades are not permitted.** Once a tier is purchased, the Client cannot downgrade to a lower tier.
- The Client may **cancel their current service** and purchase a new lower-tier service, but:
  - The original purchase is **not refunded**.
  - The Client must go through the **full setup process** again.
  - Data migration from the old instance to the new one is **not guaranteed**.

## 6. FORCE MAJEURE & EXCEPTIONAL CIRCUMSTANCES

### 6.1 Force Majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays or failures in performance due to events beyond their reasonable control, including:

- Natural disasters (floods, earthquakes, pandemics)
- Internet backbone outages
- Government actions or regulations
- Third-party service failures (Supabase, Vercel, Brevo)

In such cases, Vitharn will:
- Provide **regular updates** on the situation.
- Offer **service extensions** to compensate for downtime.
- Provide **extended data export windows** if the service is permanently affected.

### 6.2 Vitharn Business Closure

In the unlikely event that Vitharn ERP Services **ceases operations**:

1. Vitharn will provide **at least 90 days' notice** to all active clients.
2. All clients will receive a **complete data export** in CSV format.
3. Final tier clients will also receive **Tally XML exports**.
4. Vitharn will provide **migration assistance** to help clients transition to alternative solutions.
5. Any **unused annual subscription fees** will be refunded on a pro-rata basis.

### 7.1 Good Faith Negotiation

In the event of a dispute regarding refunds or cancellations:

1. Both parties agree to attempt **good-faith negotiation** for at least **14 days**.
2. All communications should be in **writing** (email to `vitarn.dev@gmail.com`).
3. Vitharn will provide a **written response** to any dispute within **5 business days**.

### 7.2 Arbitration

If good-faith negotiation fails:
- Disputes will be resolved through **arbitration in Hyderabad, Telangana**.
- The arbitration will be conducted in **English**.
- The arbitrator's decision is **final and binding**.
- This Agreement is governed by the laws of **Telangana, India**.
