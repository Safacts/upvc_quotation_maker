# 📋 Business Documents — How to Use

## What Each File Is For

### 01_Client_Contract.md
- **What:** Legal agreement between Vitharn ERP Services and the client
- **When to use:** Before starting work with any new client
- **How:** Fill in `[bracketed placeholders]` with client details → Send as PDF for signature
- **Who:** Aadi sends to client, both sign

### 02_Kickoff_Call.md
- **What:** Agenda for the first Google Meet with a new client
- **When to use:** After contract is signed, before setup begins
- **How:** Share with client before the call, follow the agenda
- **Who:** Aadi/Madhan lead the call

### 03_Invoice.md
- **What:** Template for billing clients
- **When to use:** After work is completed or milestone reached
- **How:** Fill in client details + amounts → Send as PDF
- **Who:** Aadi sends to client

### 04_Thank_You.md
- **What:** Welcome message after onboarding
- **When to use:** After client's ERP instance is ready
- **How:** Send via email or WhatsApp after setup
- **Who:** Madhan sends to client

### 05_Invoice_PDF_Spec.md
- **What:** Technical spec for the PDF invoice generator
- **When to use:** When updating invoice design or adding features
- **How:** Reference for developers, not for clients
- **Who:** Aadi/Dev team only

### 06_Desktop_Dashboard_Pilot_Agreement.md
- **What:** Agreement for clients testing the desktop console
- **When to use:** Before giving KPR (or any client) console access
- **How:** Fill placeholders → Client signs before getting access
- **Who:** Aadi sends to client
